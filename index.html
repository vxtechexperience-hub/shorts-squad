Turn long gaming videos into vertical Shorts.

Shorts Squad is a desktop app for Windows made for the Vice City 360 BR channel. It finds the best moments of GTA 6 commentary and gameplay videos, cuts them into vertical clips with animated captions, and sends each clip to the channel's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts.

Find the moment

The video is transcribed on the computer and an editor agent picks the parts with a strong hook and a complete idea.

Make the clip

Each part becomes a 9:16 clip (1080×1920) with word-by-word captions, the hook as a title and the channel name.

Send it

Approved clips go to the channel's accounts. On TikTok they arrive as drafts: the creator reviews and posts each one in the TikTok app.

How it works with TikTok

  1. In Settings → Integrations, the creator clicks Connect TikTok and logs in on TikTok's official authorization page (Login Kit). The app asks only for user.info.basic, to show which account is connected, and video.upload, to send drafts.
  2. When a clip is ready, Shorts Squad uploads the video file to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, upload to inbox).
  3. TikTok sends a notification to the creator, who opens it in the TikTok app, edits the caption and decides whether to post. Shorts Squad never posts on TikTok by itself.
  4. The creator can disconnect TikTok in the app at any time; the access tokens are deleted from the computer.

Shorts Squad runs only on the creator's own computer. It has no server and does not collect data from other people. Tokens are stored encrypted with Windows secure storage.
